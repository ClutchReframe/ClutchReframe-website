Shared by Both Products

Built around the creator, not the crop.

Both products use local visual analysis on Windows to reframe League of Legends and Dota 2 action for creator-ready vertical output.

Action-aware framing

Local visual analysis follows important League of Legends and Dota 2 action and helps keep key combat details within the output frame—without manual keyframing.

Vertical-first creation

Clips creates share-ready 9:16 videos after the match; Live creates an automatically reframed vertical OBS output while the match is live.

Local gameplay processing

Highlight videos, live gameplay frames, and reframing decisions are processed on your Windows PC; gameplay footage is not uploaded by either product.

One Goal, Two Workflows

Create on your timeline.

Clips captures during play. After the match, choose a highlight and click Generate Vertical to create a 9:16 video. Live analyzes and reframes the broadcast as the match happens.
